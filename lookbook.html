Choosing Your Lehenga
for Every Skin Tone

Understanding which colours complement your skin tone is the first step towards looking and feeling extraordinary. Our colour-to-skin guide breaks it down beautifully.

  1. Warm undertones — look best in champagne, terracotta, antique gold and deep maroon.
  2. Cool undertones — look stunning in jewel tones, royal blue, emerald and dusty rose.
  3. Neutral undertones — lucky you! Almost every palette works — choose for mood.
  4. Fabric weight matters — lighter georgette for summer, richer silk for evenings and celebrations.

Style Guide

The Modern Man's Guide
to Indian Formal Wear

From choosing between a bandhgala and a sherwani, to pairing the right footwear and pocket square — here is everything the well-dressed Indian man needs to know.

  1. Sherwani — ideal for groom occasions and wedding receptions. Rich embroidery is expected.
  2. Bandhgala — elegant yet versatile. Perfect for sangeets, cocktails and family functions.
  3. Nehru jacket — contemporary and sharp, pairs beautifully with slim churidars or trousers.
  4. Footwear rule — juttis for traditional events; pointed loafers for fusion occasions.

Mastering Indo Western
The Art of Fusion Dressing

The beauty of Indo-Western fashion lies in its boundary-defying nature. But great fusion takes thought, proportion awareness and an understanding of where tradition ends and modernity begins.

  1. Lead with one — let either the Indian or Western element dominate; balance, not confusion.
  2. Proportion play — a structured jacket with flowing palazzo is timeless and always works.
  3. Embellishment discipline — one statement piece per outfit. Over-embellishment kills the look.
  4. Footwear bridges worlds — block heels or strappy sandals complete the fusion seamlessly.
